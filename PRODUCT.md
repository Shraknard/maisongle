# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Strictly personal use. The sole user is the developer/creator looking for an efficient way to aggregate and analyze real estate listings.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a central hub that aggregates real estate listings from multiple platforms (SeLoger, PAP, Bien'ici, Leboncoin), eliminating the need to browse each site individually. Success means catching relevant listings quickly, filtering out the noise, and having immediate access to external enrichment data (like risk analysis, neighborhood data, and AI insights) right alongside the listing.

## Positioning
The only real estate tool that pulls listings from every major French platform into a single feed and automatically enriches them with vital third-party data (Géorisques, market prices, AI analysis) that standard portals hide or ignore.

## Brand Personality
Utilitarian, data-rich, clean, and modern. A highly functional dashboard that prioritizes information density without feeling cluttered.

## Anti-references
No specific anti-references provided, but generally aims to avoid the fragmented, ad-heavy, or artificially limited experiences of mainstream real estate portals.

## Design Principles
- **Density with Clarity:** Present as much enriched data as possible without overwhelming the eye.
- **Data First:** The listing and its enriched context are the heroes; everything else gets out of the way.
- **Efficient Workflows:** Actions like hiding properties or saving favorites should be instantaneous and frictionless.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Support for both Light and Dark mode is required.
